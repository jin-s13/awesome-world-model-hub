Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • EgoForge, an egocentric goal-directed world simulator that generates coherent first-person video rollouts from minimal static inputs: a single egocentric image, a high-level instruction, and an optional auxiliary exocentric view.
  • GRAFT, a trajectory-level diffusion refinement method that uses positive and negative rollout distributions derived from goal, temporal, scene-consistency, and perceptual rewards to steer the diffusion velocity field toward coherent, goal-complete egocentric simulations.
  • Operates without dense supervision such as camera trajectories, long video prefixes, or synchronized multi-camera capture, unlike prior egocentric approaches.

Methodology

EgoForge takes a single egocentric image, a high-level instruction, and an optional auxiliary exocentric view to generate first-person video rollouts. It employs GRAFT, a trajectory-level diffusion refinement method that steers the diffusion velocity field using positive and negative rollout distributions derived from goal, temporal, scene-consistency, and perceptual rewards.

Key Results

Extensive experiments show EgoForge achieves consistent gains in semantic alignment, geometric stability, and motion fidelity over strong baselines, and performs robustly in real-world smart-glasses experiments.
