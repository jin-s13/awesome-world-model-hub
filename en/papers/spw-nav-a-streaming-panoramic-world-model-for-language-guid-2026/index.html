Methodology

SPW-Nav takes a single panorama and language movement instructions, interpreting each instruction in the previously generated panorama as camera motion. It uses spherical rotation decoupling to apply rotation exactly on the sphere, pose-aligned conditioning to keep translation inputs bounded over long streams, and a multi-term memory with a few-step generator for continuation as instructions change. The approach is evaluated using the newly built SPW-NavSet dataset of panoramic videos with camera trajectories and verified instructions, comparing against prior panoramic generators on camera-following accuracy and video quality.

Key Results

Language-driven SPW-Nav outperforms prior panoramic generators in camera-following accuracy and video quality, and it supports on-the-fly instruction switching while generating one minute of 2K 360-degree video in real time from a single panorama.
