OuroWorld: Bringing Any 3D World Alive as Diverse, Endlessly Looping 3D Cinemagraphs

arXiv 2026 35.2 method

TLDR

OuroWorld turns static 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes into looping 3D cinemagraphs with diverse motion, using VLM-guided video generation and periodic 4DGS, outperforming baselines on 39 scenes.

Reasoning

The paper proposes a novel mask-free framework for animating static 3D scenes with looping motion, backed by multiple evaluations including user studies. However, the method depends on imperfect generated reference videos and ground-truth-free metrics, and its generalizability to complex real-world dynamics remains uncertain.

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Mask-free framework that turns any static 3D Gaussian Splatting scene into a 3D cinemagraph with diverse, endlessly looping motion from any viewpoint.
  • Vision-language model guided reference video synthesis that infers plausible dynamics and is lifted and completed into multi-view videos.
  • Inconsistency-Robust Periodic 4DGS representation with a Fourier-series deformation field that guarantees looping by construction.
  • Grounded Drift Field anchored at the reference view to absorb cross-view inconsistency in imperfect lifted supervision.
  • Extends dynamic scene modeling beyond prior Eulerian fluid-like motion to general deformation, object motion, and illumination change.
  • Ground-truth-free evaluation protocol covering vividness, naturalness, loop seam coherence, and scene quality.

Methodology

OuroWorld takes a static 3D Gaussian Splatting scene and uses a vision-language model to infer plausible dynamics, guiding a video model to synthesize a reference video that is then lifted and completed into multi-view videos. It learns dynamic scenes with Inconsistency-Robust Periodic 4DGS, where a Fourier-series deformation field guarantees looping by construction and a Grounded Drift Field anchored at the reference view absorbs cross-view inconsistency. Evaluation uses 39 reconstructed and generated scenes with ground-truth-free metrics and user studies.

Key Results

On 39 reconstructed and generated scenes, OuroWorld outperforms all baselines and wins 70.8%-99.0% of user-study comparisons across vividness, naturalness, loop seam coherence, and scene quality.

Limitations

  • Learns from imperfect supervision because the reference video and lifted multi-view videos are synthesized rather than ground-truth observations.
