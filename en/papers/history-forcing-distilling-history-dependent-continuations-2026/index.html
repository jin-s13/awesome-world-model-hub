Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Proposes History Forcing, an on-policy distillation framework for few-step autoregressive video generators that strengthens long-range historical supervision.
  • Decouples historical supervision by using the residual between teacher predictions with and without informative historical context.
  • Obtains historical context from earlier generated chunks with less accumulated drift or from fixed-view teacher rollouts that keep the scene visible while the student looks away.
  • Forms a corrected distillation target by weighting and adding the historical residual to the score or velocity prediction.

Methodology

The method distills a few-step autoregressive video diffusion student on-policy using a teacher. For each prediction, it computes a teacher residual between predictions with and without informative historical context, then adds a weighted version of this residual to the score or velocity prediction to create a corrected target. Evaluation uses VBench and VBench-Long for generation quality and PlayWorld and MemoBench for out-of-sight world modeling.

Key Results

History Forcing achieves state-of-the-art among 1.3B models, with a VBench quality score of 85.07 for 15s video generation and a VBench-Long aggregate score of 79.82 for 60s generation. It improves Evolution Quality from 2.35 to 2.70 on PlayWorld and Object Consistency from 58.43 to 62.18 on MemoBench.
