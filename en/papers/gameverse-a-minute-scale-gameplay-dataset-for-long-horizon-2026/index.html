GameVerse: A Minute-Scale Gameplay Dataset for Long-Horizon Interactive World Modeling

TLDR

Reasoning

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • GameVerse dataset: 69.7K clips, 1,655 hours, 349.6 million frames of diverse first- and third-person gameplay, including 1,358 hours of continuous two-minute sequences, an order of magnitude longer than prior datasets
  • Unified annotation pipeline combining scene-consistent segmentation, quality filtering, camera pose estimation, and event-based caption annotation
  • Minute-scale temporally stable camera trajectories that remain coherent across full clips
  • Event-aligned captions that separate static scene context from dynamic foreground events instead of using fixed time windows

Methodology

GameVerse is constructed from 69.7K gameplay clips across first- and third-person views, processed through a unified pipeline of scene-consistent segmentation, quality filtering, camera pose estimation, and event-based caption annotation. Each clip is paired with a temporally stable camera trajectory and captions aligned to event boundaries. The authors compare GameVerse to existing public datasets on scale, diversity, and annotation quality, and train interactive world models on GameVerse to assess temporal horizon extension.

Key Results

Analysis shows that GameVerse surpasses existing public datasets in scale, diversity, and annotation quality. Training on GameVerse substantially extends the temporal horizon of interactive world models.
