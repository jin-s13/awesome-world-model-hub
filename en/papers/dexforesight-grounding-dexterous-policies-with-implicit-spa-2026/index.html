Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Learns spatial foresight implicitly by injecting dynamics-aware world representations into the latent space of a dexterous policy, rather than using explicit future prediction.
  • Uses an action-conditioned spatial world model trained on offline scene depth, kinematic hand geometry, and demonstration trajectories to predict 3D hand-point motion and visual feature changes, including occluded finger motion.
  • Freezes the world model and aligns policy queries with predictive world queries to transfer learned spatial dynamics into policy latents for action generation.
  • Enables deployment with only the policy, using RGB observations, language, and robot state without depth input or explicit future prediction.

Methodology

DexForesight trains an action-conditioned spatial world model on offline scene depth, kinematic hand geometry, and demonstration trajectories to predict 3D hand-point motion and visual feature changes. The world model is then frozen, and policy queries are aligned with its predictive world queries to transfer spatial dynamics into policy latents and condition action generation. At deployment, only the policy is retained and operates on RGB observations, language, and robot state, without depth input or explicit future prediction.

Key Results

On 11 DexJoCo simulation tasks, DexForesight improves overall success rate from 49.4% for the base policy to 60.5%. On three real-robot dexterous manipulation tasks, it achieves 58.3% success compared with 50.0% for the strongest baseline.
