Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Decouples panoramic world evolution from perspective observation synthesis in a streaming video world model.
  • Constructs a 360-degree scene prior from a single perspective image using a pretrained panorama expansion model.
  • Evolves scenes as camera-conditioned panoramic latent states and renders requested perspective video latents through a latent viewport renderer.
  • Adds a perspective refiner for detail restoration, artifact suppression, and super-resolution.
  • Adapts panoramic generation to chunk-autoregressive generation and distills panoramic generation and perspective refinement into few-step processes for efficient streaming.
  • Introduces MUGEN, a large-scale real-world panoramic video dataset with 1,318 hours of at least 4K video and rich semantic and geometric annotations.

Methodology

AlayaVista begins with a single perspective image, expands it into a 360-degree scene prior via a pretrained panorama expansion model, and evolves the scene as a camera-conditioned panoramic latent state. A latent viewport renderer maps this state to perspective video latents, while a perspective refiner restores details, suppresses artifacts, and performs super-resolution; the panoramic generator uses chunk-autoregressive generation and both panoramic generation and perspective refinement are distilled into few-step processes.

Key Results

The abstract does not report quantitative experimental results or benchmark metrics; it highlights the MUGEN dataset scale of 1,318 hours of real-world panoramic video at resolutions of at least 4K.
