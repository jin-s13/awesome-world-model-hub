Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces InfluenceField, an intervention-aware latent field placed between the visual encoder and language decoder that lifts patch features into a continuous spatial representation, propagates directed influence over multiple steps, and predicts local intervention effects via a shared transition operator.
  • Defines a joint training objective combining language modeling, cross-environment invariance, counterfactual rollout supervision, and structural regularization.
  • Provides theoretical identifiability results for a nonlinear finite-basis population model: target-aligned interventional supervision plus a one-step separation condition restricts representations to within-location reparameterizations and recovers the directed dependency graph exactly.
  • Provides a linear specialization with an exact partial-coverage characterization and finite-loss stability bound, plus field analysis of spatial coefficient intervention profiles and a shared-channel calibration result.
  • Demonstrates large empirical gains on CausalVQA, with capacity-matched baselines and structural controls attributing improvements to causal objectives rather than added capacity.

Methodology

InfluenceField is inserted between the visual encoder and language decoder, lifting patch features into a continuous spatial latent field where a shared transition operator propagates directed influence over multiple steps and predicts local intervention effects. Training jointly optimizes language modeling, cross-environment invariance, counterfactual rollout supervision, and structural regularization. The approach is evaluated on CausalVQA against the backbone, capacity-matched baselines, and structural controls, while theoretical properties are analyzed in nonlinear finite-basis population and linear settings.

Key Results

On CausalVQA, InfluenceField improves overall accuracy over its backbone by 13.1 percentage points, with the largest gains on planning and hypothetical categories. Capacity-matched baselines and structural controls attribute robustness and factual-counterfactual consistency gains to the causal objectives rather than added capacity; identifiability analysis shows exact directed dependency graph recovery under the stated conditions.
