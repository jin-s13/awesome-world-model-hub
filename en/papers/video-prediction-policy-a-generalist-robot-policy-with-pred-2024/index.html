Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Using video diffusion models to produce visual representations that capture both static and dynamic information for robot policy learning.
  • Proposing Video Prediction Policy (VPP) that learns an implicit inverse dynamics model conditioned on predicted future representations from VDMs.
  • Fine-tuning a pre-trained video foundation model on robot datasets and internet human manipulation data to improve future prediction precision.

Methodology

VPP uses a video diffusion model to predict future frames and extract representations that include current static and future dynamic information. An implicit inverse dynamics model is learned conditioned on these predicted future representations. The video foundation model is fine-tuned on robot datasets and internet human manipulation data.

Key Results

VPP achieves a 18.6% relative improvement on the Calvin ABC-D generalization benchmark over previous state-of-the-art, and a 31.6% increase in success rates for complex real-world dexterous manipulation tasks.
