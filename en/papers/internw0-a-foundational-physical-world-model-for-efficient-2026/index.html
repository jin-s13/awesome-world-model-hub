Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • First instantiation of the InternW physical world model series, built around omnimodal interfaces, asynchronous multi-frequency processing, and local physical modeling under partial observations and external influences
  • Asymmetric video-action architecture that jointly learns future visual dynamics and continuous robot control using flow matching
  • High-capacity video expert for longer-horizon predictive context paired with a lightweight action expert operating at a faster timescale
  • Reuses layerwise K/V and adapts to newly observed states through observation-conditioned context routing instead of regenerating the future for every action update
  • Domain-specific interfaces and soft prompts supporting heterogeneous embodiments
  • Contact-aware post-training incorporating force and tactile signals for contact-rich manipulation
  • EgoLab dataset, a 275-hour real-laboratory egocentric dataset, included within approximately 7,200 hours of heterogeneous robot and egocentric training data

Methodology

InternW0 uses an asymmetric video-action architecture with flow matching to jointly learn future visual dynamics and continuous robot control. A high-capacity video expert supplies longer-horizon predictive context while a lightweight action expert operates at a faster timescale, reusing layerwise K/V with observation-conditioned context routing. The model is trained on about 7,200 hours of heterogeneous robot and egocentric data, including EgoLab, with contact-aware post-training using force and tactile signals.

Key Results

Evaluation spans simulation benchmarks and real-world scientific tasks, including a 15-stage metal-organic framework synthesis workflow and 5-stage contact- and force-aware dexterous manipulation for general-purpose quantitative pipetting.
