Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • ActWorld-JEPA: a joint-embedding formulation for candidate-based end-to-end autonomous driving that connects latent prediction to candidate selection.
  • Plan-Conditioned JEPA (PC-JEPA): uses global and step-wise trajectory intentions to guide recurrent latent prediction and combines predicted future with the current scene via gated cross-attention.
  • Relational Residual Reranking (R^3): models agreement and discrepancy between plan and future representations under planning supervision, yielding bounded, gated residual corrections to base planning scores.
  • Candidate evaluation at inference uses only observed context and proposed trajectories, avoiding pixel reconstruction.

Methodology

ActWorld-JEPA combines PC-JEPA for action-conditioned latent future prediction with R^3 to rerank candidate driving plans. PC-JEPA conditions recurrent latent prediction on global and step-wise trajectory intentions and fuses predicted future with current scene via gated cross-attention; R^3 then applies planning-supervised residual corrections. The approach is evaluated on NAVSIM v1, NAVSIM v2, and nuScenes using PDMS/EPDMS, mean L2 error, and collision rate metrics.

Key Results

ActWorld-JEPA reports 94.1 PDMS on NAVSIM v1 navtest, 91.6 and 42.7 EPDMS on NAVSIM v2 navtest and navhard, and a nuScenes mean L2 error of 1.20 m with a predicted collision rate of 0.76% over 1-6 s.
