WAM-Sim0.1: World Anchored Memory Meets Online RL for Consistent and Realistic Driving Simulation

arXiv 2026 65.1 method, application

TLDR

WAM-Sim is a driving world model with persistent hierarchical memory and online flow-matching RL to reduce drift and improve fidelity in long-horizon driving simulation.

Reasoning

The paper introduces a novel architecture combining persistent multi-scale memory with online RL for world model consistency, showing strong quantitative results on nuScenes. Weaknesses include limited visible evidence on interactive capabilities and reliance on self-collected data for long-rollout claims.

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces a persistent hierarchical memory with pose-aligned structural tokens, read-only scene tokens, and write-once instance tokens to anchor road topology, background appearance, and traffic participant identity.
  • Proposes online post-training via flow-matching reinforcement learning with hierarchy-aligned visual rewards and a vision-language model physical judge.
  • Uses invariant memory to confine policy optimization within anchored context, helping suppress semantic collapse and reward hacking.
  • Demonstrates long-horizon mitigation of structural and identity drift in minute-scale rollouts.

Methodology

WAM-Sim structures persistent context across three temporal and semantic memory scales and is optimized through online flow-matching reinforcement learning on self-generated rollouts. The reinforcement learning objective uses hierarchy-aligned visual rewards and a vision-language model physical judge. Evaluation is conducted on the nuScenes benchmark and self-collected minute-scale data using generation fidelity, downstream perception, scene similarity, and identity coherence metrics.

Key Results

On nuScenes, WAM-Sim achieves FID=3.47 and FVD=25.72, with downstream perception of 24.16 mAP and 71.23 road mIoU. On minute-scale rollouts, it substantially mitigates structural and identity drift while preserving high scene similarity and subject identity coherence.
