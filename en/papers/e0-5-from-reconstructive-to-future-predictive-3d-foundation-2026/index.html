Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Adopts a 3D foundation model as the primary backbone, making 3D geometry the native representation for both world modeling and action prediction instead of using geometry as auxiliary input or prediction target.
  • Explicitly predicts future 3D states together with actions, enabling the model to capture geometric world dynamics beyond action-only modeling or future video prediction.

Methodology

E0.5 is a geometry-native world-action model built directly on 3D foundation models, using 3D geometry as the native representation for world modeling and action prediction. It is evaluated on RoboTwin 2.0 without embodied-data pretraining, then pretrained on more than 160k robot trajectories from AgiBotWorld-Beta to jointly learn robot actions and future geometry. Real-world manipulation success rates are measured on AgileX Piper and Zerith H1.

Key Results

On RoboTwin 2.0, E0.5 achieves success rates of 89.90% under clean and 50.09% under randomized settings without embodied-data pretraining. After embodied pretraining, mean real-world manipulation success rates improve by approximately 15 percentage points on AgileX Piper and 7 percentage points on Zerith H1.
