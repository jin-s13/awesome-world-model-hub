Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Streaming causal autoregressive diffusion architecture that synthesizes audio chunks in real time with a low real-time factor of 0.41
  • Audio-centric captioning pipeline with chunk-indexed prompt scheduling for interactive mid-stream control of sound events
  • High-quality stereo supervision curated from diverse stereo and ambisonic data for spatially aligned stereo synthesis

Methodology

WorldSonus is an interactive video-to-audio framework that uses a streaming causal autoregressive diffusion model to generate audio chunks in real time. It incorporates an audio-centric captioning pipeline with chunk-indexed prompt scheduling for dynamic, mid-stream sound control and leverages stereo supervision from diverse stereo and ambisonic data for spatial alignment. The approach is evaluated on open-domain video-to-audio benchmarks against state-of-the-art bidirectional models.

Key Results

WorldSonus achieves a real-time factor of 0.41 for audio generation and matches or outperforms state-of-the-art bidirectional models on open-domain video-to-audio benchmarks in both acoustic quality and spatial alignment.
