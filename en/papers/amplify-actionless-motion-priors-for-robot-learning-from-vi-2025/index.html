Amplify: Actionless Motion Priors for Robot Learning from Videos

arXiv 2025 59.9 method

TLDR

Amplify learns actionless motion priors from videos via discrete keypoint trajectory tokens, decoupling visual dynamics prediction from action inference to improve policy learning and video prediction.

Reasoning

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces Amplify, a framework that leverages large-scale action-free video data by encoding visual dynamics into compact, discrete motion tokens derived from keypoint trajectories.
  • Proposes a modular approach that separates visual motion prediction from action inference, decoupling learning what motion defines a task from how robots can perform it.
  • Trains a forward dynamics model on abundant action-free videos and an inverse dynamics model on limited action-labeled examples, allowing independent scaling.
  • Demonstrates latent dynamics learned by Amplify enhance video prediction quality beyond robotic control.

Methodology

Amplify encodes visual dynamics from keypoint trajectories into compact, discrete motion tokens. It trains a forward dynamics model on large-scale action-free videos and an inverse dynamics model on a limited set of action-labeled examples. The framework is evaluated on point track prediction, downstream policy learning, generalization to LIBERO tasks, and video prediction.

Key Results

The dynamics model achieves over 2x better point track prediction accuracy than the prior state-of-the-art. In downstream policy learning, Amplify enables 1.2-2.2x success rate improvement in low-data regimes, 1.4x average improvement from action-free human videos, and the first generalization to LIBERO tasks with zero in-distribution action data.
