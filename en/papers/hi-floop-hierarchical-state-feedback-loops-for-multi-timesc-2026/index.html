Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Branch-consistent multi-timescale state-feedback framework with eight scene-level Worlds representing joint hypotheses, where all agents share the selected World identity throughout an 8-second rollout
  • Hierarchical decomposition into 8-second Goal anchoring intent, 2-second Preview coordinating interactions, and 1-second Control producing physical motion, with 0.5-second commits feeding back only executed prefixes as new facts
  • Joint Preview Interaction (JPI) induces a sparse directed future graph from Preview and uses conflict probabilities and signed arrival-time differences to gate interaction refinement
  • Prefix-frozen A-to-B cascade for generated-state recovery transfers typed physical state, admissible context, and branch index but no latent state from Model A to independent Model B

Methodology

HI-FLOOP models multi-agent traffic futures with eight shared scene-level World branches and decomposes generation into hierarchical 8-second Goal, 2-second Preview, and 1-second Control loops with 0.5-second state-feedback commits. It includes a Joint Preview Interaction module that gates interaction refinement via conflict probabilities and signed arrival-time differences, and a prefix-frozen A-to-B cascade for generated-state recovery that transfers typed physical state, admissible context, and branch index without latent state. Evaluation is reported on the full H-D public-validation split of 955 scenarios using the official evaluator and agent-centric oracle metrics.

Key Results

One complete S1 run on the full H-D public-validation split of 955 scenarios yields an Overall score of 0.689987 with the official evaluator; under agent-centric oracle evaluation, HI-FLOOP achieves oracle-minADE@8 of 1.196636 m over the 8-second horizon and 0.526 m over the 6-second horizon.
