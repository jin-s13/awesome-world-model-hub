Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • ActionRoPE: a parameter-free action interface that converts action-driven planar camera displacement into world-coordinate rotary positions, so tokens corresponding to the same world location retain the same positional address across time.
  • Coordinate-Conditioned Noise Scheduling (CNS): token-wise denoising schedules based on the known-new spatial asymmetry, allowing observed regions to stabilize earlier while newly revealed regions are synthesized with reference to them.
  • Identification of a spatial asymmetry in planar-camera generation: regions covered by the initial observation are geometrically known, while newly revealed regions remain to be synthesized.

Methodology

ActionRoPE represents actions as planar camera displacements and encodes them directly as world-coordinate rotary positions rather than using feature conditioning. Coordinate-Conditioned Noise Scheduling assigns token-wise denoising schedules according to whether tokens correspond to known observed regions or newly revealed regions. The method is evaluated against four representative action-conditioning mechanisms using video backbones from 1.3B to 33B parameters, including out-of-distribution scenes, with metrics of commanded-motion faithfulness and revisit drift.

Key Results

ActionRoPE follows commanded motion more faithfully than the compared action-conditioning mechanisms and reduces revisit drift by 66% over the strongest baseline. The same interface transfers across video backbones from 1.3B to 33B parameters and to out-of-distribution scenes.
