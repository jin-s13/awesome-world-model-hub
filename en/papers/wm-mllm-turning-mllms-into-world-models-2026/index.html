WM^MLLM: Turning MLLMs into World Models

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces WM^MLLM, claimed as the first interactive world model built on multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for persistent, editable world generation from a single image.
  • Keeps world state outside every generator as a panorama with a revision history, rather than storing it in generator context, to prevent revisits from drifting or edits disappearing.
  • Uses MLLMs as writers that turn each request into operations on world state, select where local edits are written, and check candidate states before committing them.
  • Separates generative roles: image generators only propose views and video only presents them, while the MLLM maintains the persistent editable state.
  • Provides construction-time guarantees: revisiting at the same revision reproduces the earlier view exactly, local edits leave pixels outside their region unchanged, and each request starts from the previous result.

Methodology

WM^MLLM maintains a persistent panorama with revision history as external world state, where MLLMs translate user requests into state operations, choose local edit regions, and verify candidate states. Image generators and video are used only to propose and present views. The model is evaluated on MBench-A, CompBench, and WBench against multiple baselines using environment-recovery, camera round-trip, local-edit completion, and pixel-invariance metrics.

Key Results

Against six baselines on MBench-A, WM^MLLM achieves the highest mean on all four environment-recovery metrics (Epipolar 91.5 versus 0.1 to 74.0) and completes the most camera round trips (85.3% versus at most 50.9%). It performs best in all 15 CompBench columns against 15 baselines; selecting the edit region after generation raises local-edit completion from 34.8% to 83.0%, and across 258 WBench and MBench-A items all 46 revisits recover earlier views and all 97 check-passing local edits leave outside-region pixels unchanged.
