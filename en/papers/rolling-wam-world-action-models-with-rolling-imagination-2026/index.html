Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Distributes joint video-action denoising across successive replanning cycles instead of denoising the full horizon at each cycle
  • Maintains a sliding window of video-action chunks at staggered noise levels
  • Uses a rolling noise schedule that fully denoises the imminent action chunk for execution while partially refining farther-future chunks
  • Carries an evolving visual-action context across chunk boundaries as retained future chunks continue denoising with new camera observations

Methodology

Rolling-WAM introduces a rolling formulation of World Action Models in which a sliding window of video-action chunks is maintained at staggered noise levels. At each replanning step, a rolling noise schedule fully denoises the imminent action chunk for execution and partially refines future chunks, while newly observed camera frames advance the window and retained chunks continue denoising. The method is evaluated on LIBERO, RoboTwin, and a real-world Unitree G1 humanoid against standard joint WAMs using manipulation performance and replanning speedup.

Key Results

Rolling-WAM achieves competitive manipulation performance on LIBERO, RoboTwin, and a real-world Unitree G1 humanoid. It provides a 4.5x steady-state replanning speedup over standard joint WAMs by avoiding denoising the entire prediction horizon from scratch.
