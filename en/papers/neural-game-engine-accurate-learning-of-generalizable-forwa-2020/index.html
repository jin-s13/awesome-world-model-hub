Neural Game Engine: Accurate learning of generalizable forward models from pixels.

arXiv 2020 57.1 method

TLDR

Introduces Neural Game Engine, a forward model learned from pixels that generalizes across game levels, achieving perfect predictions on many GVGAI games.

Reasoning

The paper presents a novel approach for learning game forward models from pixels with strong generalization and empirical results on 10 games. Weaknesses include limited scope to deterministic games and potential scalability concerns, but the public release and clear methodology are strengths.

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Neural Game Engine: a method to learn accurate forward models directly from pixels, building on the Neural GPU architecture.
  • Models generalize to different size game levels than those seen during training without loss of accuracy.

Methodology

The Neural Game Engine is built upon the Neural GPU to learn forward models from raw pixels. It is evaluated on 10 deterministic General Video Game AI games, learning to predict both next-frame pixels and rewards. Pre-trained models are provided via the OpenAI Gym interface.

Key Results

On 10 deterministic GVG-AI games, many models learned perfectly, achieving accurate pixel-level predictions and reward predictions, and generalizing to unseen level sizes.
