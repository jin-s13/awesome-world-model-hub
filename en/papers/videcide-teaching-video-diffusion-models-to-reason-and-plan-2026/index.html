Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • ViDecide, a decision-centric framework that trains video diffusion models to predict state transitions causally and decide when to stop using a decision-focused training schedule
  • Corrective Fine-tuning (CFT), which corrects failed decisions by mapping their noise to the model's own successful continuations without policy-gradient optimization
  • A new benchmark for multi-step robot planning

Methodology

ViDecide trains video diffusion models to treat solutions as chains of decisions rather than fixed-length clips, predicting state transitions causally and deciding when to stop. Corrective Fine-tuning maps failed decisions' noise to the model's own successful continuations for stable improvement without policy-gradient optimization. The framework is evaluated on abstract games, planning tasks, and a new multi-step robot planning benchmark.

Key Results

ViDecide achieves an average strict success rate of 83.8%, compared to 23.3% for standard fine-tuning and 67.8% for the strongest prior method. It also generalizes to larger puzzles and unseen layouts.
