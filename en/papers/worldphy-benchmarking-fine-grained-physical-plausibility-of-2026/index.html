Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces WorldPhy, a benchmark for fine-grained physical plausibility evaluation of world models in robotics, with 300 tasks spanning diverse scenes, task categories, viewpoints, and robot configurations.
  • Collects over 12K high-quality human annotations and uses them to train WorldPhyReward, which provides scores and reasons across six fine-grained physical dimensions and an instruction-following dimension.
  • Conducts systematic evaluation of 13 frontier open- and closed-source world models using the proposed benchmark and reward model.

Methodology

WorldPhy contains 300 tasks, each consisting of an initial-frame image and a task instruction. Over 12K human annotations are collected and used to train WorldPhyReward to score and explain outputs across six fine-grained physical dimensions plus instruction following. WorldPhyReward is compared with general VLMs and existing reward models for human-rating alignment, and 13 frontier world models are evaluated on the benchmark.

Key Results

WorldPhyReward better aligns with human ratings than general VLMs and existing reward models. Systematic evaluation of 13 frontier world models shows they struggle to consistently maintain fine-grained physical plausibility across different robotics scenarios.
