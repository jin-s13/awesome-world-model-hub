Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Revisits kinodynamic sampling-based motion planning for visual world models by using the learned model as the propagator of a tree search in latent space.
  • Shows that sampling strategies from high-dimensional configuration-space planning apply directly to world-model latent spaces, enabling expansion through intermediate states that myopic optimizers miss.
  • Introduces two latent-space exploration strategies: nearest-neighbor-guided RRT and projection-based KPIECE.
  • Uses GPU-native batched expansion for efficient device utilization during planning.

Methodology

The method uses an off-the-shelf DINO-WM visual world model as the propagator for sampling-based tree search in latent space. It adapts kinodynamic planning strategies, specifically nearest-neighbor-guided RRT and projection-based KPIECE, with GPU-native batched expansion. Evaluation compares against CEM and MPPI on four simulated domains and a real-world block-pushing task using success rate and planning time.

Key Results

The proposed approaches match or exceed the success rate of CEM and MPPI at up to 13.8x lower planning time in simulation, and improve success rate by 20% on a real robot block-pushing task.
