Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces RynnWorld-Latent, a cross-embodiment video world model conditioned on universal latent actions from RynnLAM as a shared interface across human videos, robot demonstrations, and simulation rollouts.
  • Uses global action tokens with an identity-initialized local pathway to align actions with future video latents in a pretrained video transformer.
  • Enables joint pretraining on heterogeneous interaction data without requiring a common native action space.
  • Provides two data-generation routes: digital teleoperation using target robot commands and latent-action synthesis transferring source motion to edited initial frames with retargeted pseudo robot actions.

Methodology

RynnWorld-Latent is built on a pretrained video transformer and combines global action tokens with an identity-initialized local pathway that aligns actions with future video latents. It is pretrained on 23 datasets totaling approximately 553.5M frames and adapted to target robots through post-training. The framework supports digital teleoperation and latent-action synthesis for generating action-video demonstrations.

Key Results

The model improves video prediction metrics on public benchmarks and two real-robot platforms, with qualitative action following when different action sequences share the same initial observation. Synthetic mid-training improves policy success rates on LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus, and both generation routes improve real-world policy success rates over real-demonstration-only training in the evaluated settings.
