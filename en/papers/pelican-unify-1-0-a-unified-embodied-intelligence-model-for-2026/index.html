Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces Pelican-Unify 1.0 as a unified embodied foundation model that uses a single VLM to map scenes, instructions, visual contexts, and action histories into a shared semantic space.
  • Uses the same VLM as a unified reasoning module that autoregressively produces task-, action-, and future-oriented chains of thought in one forward pass and projects the final hidden state into a dense latent variable.
  • Proposes a Unified Future Generator (UFG) that conditions on the latent variable and jointly generates future videos and future actions through two modality-specific output heads within the same denoising process.
  • Jointly backpropagates language, video, and action losses into the shared representation, enabling unified optimization of understanding, reasoning, imagination, and action instead of training isolated expert systems.

Methodology

Pelican-Unify 1.0 uses a single VLM for both understanding and reasoning, mapping diverse inputs into a shared semantic space and autoregressively outputting chains of thought plus a dense latent representation. A Unified Future Generator conditions on this latent variable and jointly generates future video and action outputs via two modality-specific heads within one denoising process. Language, video, and action losses backpropagate into the shared representation for joint optimization; training data details are not specified in the abstract.

Key Results

With a single checkpoint, Pelican-Unify 1.0 reports 64.7 on eight VLM benchmarks (best among comparable-scale models), 66.03 on WorldArena (first), and 93.5 on RoboTwin (second-best average among compared action methods). The authors interpret these results as showing the unified paradigm preserves specialist strength.
