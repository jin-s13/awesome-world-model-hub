Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • ActionSplice inference framework that formulates in-flight action editing as Counterfactual State Transport (CST).
  • A lightweight corrector transports the interrupted backbone-native representation toward the matched state induced by the revised action at the same solver step, keeping the world model and sampler frozen and resuming without replaying completed evaluations.
  • Two CST variants: CST_R updates the entire active chunk, while CST_T preserves a temporal prefix and updates only the suffix.

Methodology

The method is an inference framework for chunk-autoregressive video world models. It inserts a lightweight corrector that maps an interrupted backbone-native representation to a counterfactual state matching a revised action at the current solver step, while the world model and sampler remain frozen. Evaluation uses minWM-Wan Action2V and HY-WM1.5 with LPIPS, PSNR, SSIM, rollback-relative comparisons, and pixel-ready speedups versus waiting.

Key Results

CST_R reduces rollback-relative LPIPS by 61.5% on minWM-Wan Action2V and 75.9% on HY-WM1.5 relative to direct condition swapping; CST_T reduces suffix LPIPS by 56.1% and 77.5%, respectively, with 2.73x and 1.69x pixel-ready speedups over waiting. Under the HY-WorldPlay protocol, CST_R achieves PSNR 25.66 dB, SSIM 0.6902, and LPIPS 0.1337 against the original rollout.
