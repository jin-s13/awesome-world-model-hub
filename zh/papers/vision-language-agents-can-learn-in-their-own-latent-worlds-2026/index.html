深度分析

创新点

  • Introduces Dwell, a model-based reinforcement learning framework that reframes a vision-language model as a recurrent latent transition operator capable of predicting, adjusting, and acting in latent space.
  • Leverages the VLM's own representations and dual role as both world model and policy to learn from real interactions and latent imagination without reconstructing future observations.
  • Shows that effective world models for VLM agents need not reconstruct the future, only predict futures useful for action, substantially reducing compute required for imagination.

方法

Dwell uses a pretrained vision-language model as a recurrent latent transition operator and policy in a model-based reinforcement learning framework. The agent continually learns from real interactions to anticipate and evaluate action outcomes, and from latent imagination to improve behavior. It is evaluated across five interactive visual environments against trainable methods.

关键结果

Across five interactive visual environments, Dwell achieves the strongest overall performance among trainable methods while substantially reducing the compute required for imagination.
