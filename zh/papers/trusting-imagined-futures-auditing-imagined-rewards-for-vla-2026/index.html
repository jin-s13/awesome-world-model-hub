深度分析

创新点

  • Introduces Trusting Imagined Futures (TIF), an offline supervision selection method for VLA post-training that audits predicted rewards using execution feedback.
  • Uses a multidimensional reward model that predicts a utility score for candidate ranking and five dimensions of execution quality.
  • Introduces an auditor that uses reward predictions and uncertainty to identify overestimation patterns learned from paired imagined videos and real executions.
  • Defines an acceptance rule that keeps a candidate only when its predicted utility advantage exceeds a margin and its audit risk passes a threshold, otherwise retaining the existing supervision.

方法

TIF audits imagined rewards from video world models for VLA post-training by predicting a utility score and five execution-quality dimensions, then using an auditor trained on paired imagined videos and real executions to detect overestimated rewards. A candidate trajectory is accepted only if its utility advantage over the existing trajectory exceeds a margin and its audit risk passes a threshold; otherwise the existing real trajectory is retained. The retained real trajectories supervise behavior cloning, and evaluation spans four LIBERO suites, three training seeds, and recorded videos from three real robot tasks.

关键结果

Across four LIBERO suites and three training seeds, TIF achieves a mean success rate of 76.25%, improving over training on the existing trajectories by 3.00 percentage points with the same trajectory count and training budget; adding audit gating yields 1.47 percentage points with the reward model and advantage margin held fixed. Gains persist against advantage ranking and logistic regression risk selection at matched replacement counts, and on real robot videos TIF increases clean completion among selected trajectories from 58.33% to 70.00% across 60 candidate pools.
