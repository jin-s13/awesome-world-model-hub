深度分析

创新点

  • DeltaWAM jointly predicts visual deltas and actions using dense-anchor, sparse-delta, and action streams, avoiding dense future-frame prediction and decoupling action-conditioned dynamics from nuisance appearance variations.
  • Introduces three DeltaWAM architectures that differ in representation and computation sharing.
  • Streaming Delta Memory (SDM) updates cached anchor context with compact observed deltas, reducing heavy video-expert processing during inference.

方法

DeltaWAM builds on world-action models that transfer visual and motion priors from pretrained video generators to robot control. It uses dense-anchor, sparse-delta, and action streams to jointly predict visual deltas and actions, with three architectures varying representation and computation sharing, plus Streaming Delta Memory to update cached anchor context with compact observed deltas. Evaluation on RoboTwin and real-world bimanual manipulation compares against Fast-WAM using average success, normalized progress, training FLOPs, and inference latency/FLOPs.

关键结果

DeltaWAM with SDM improves average success over Fast-WAM from 81.3% to 85.4% in the clean setting and from 75.8% to 83.9% under visual randomization. The three architectures reduce training FLOPs by 17.78-23.77%, while SDM reduces one-step inference latency and FLOPs by 36.57% and 31.55%, respectively; real-world evaluations show the highest overall success rate and normalized progress among evaluated policies.
