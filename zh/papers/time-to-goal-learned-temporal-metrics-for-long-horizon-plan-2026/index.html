Time-to-Goal: Learned Temporal Metrics for Long-Horizon Planning with JEPA World Models

TLDR

Introduces Time-to-Goal, a learned temporal metric for JEPA world models, improving long-horizon planning by enforcing temporal ordering in latent goal costs.

评分理由

The paper identifies a clear limitation in flat JEPA world models and proposes a lightweight, reward-free metric that substantially improves planning success across multiple benchmarks. Strengths include a focused analysis and strong empirical gains; weaknesses include reliance on offline trajectories and simulated benchmark evaluations.

深度分析

创新点

  • Identifies and analyzes a mismatch in flat LeWorldModel where Euclidean goal cost does not enforce temporal ordering, causing states at different long offsets to receive similar average costs.
  • Introduces Time-to-Goal (T2G), a lightweight metric trained on latent triples from offline trajectories using a margin ranking loss that encourages smaller distances for temporally nearer states without rewards or policy labels.
  • Replaces the Euclidean planning cost with the learned T2G metric while keeping the JEPA encoder and predictor frozen.
  • Shows that learning temporal ordering for the goal cost can substantially improve latent planning even with a flat world model, approaching or surpassing hierarchical H-JEPA baselines.

方法

The paper analyzes how Euclidean goal cost in flat LeWorldModel changes with temporal separation, identifying conditions where long-offset states receive similar average costs. It introduces Time-to-Goal (T2G), a lightweight metric trained on latent triples from offline trajectories with a margin ranking loss that assigns smaller distances to temporally nearer states, requiring no rewards or policy labels. The learned metric replaces the Euclidean planning cost while the JEPA encoder and predictor remain frozen, with diagnostics on AntMaze and FourRoom and planning evaluation across four navigation and manipulation benchmarks.

关键结果

T2G preserves stronger temporal contrast and improves near-far ranking at long offsets. Across four navigation and manipulation benchmarks, T2G improves average planning success rate from 32.7% for flat LeWM to 63.0% and approaches or surpasses hierarchical H-JEPA baselines.
